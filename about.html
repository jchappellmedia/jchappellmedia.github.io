About

Hi, I'm Josh.

I make videos about building real things with AI, and this is where the files live.

Most AI content stops at the demo. The demo works, everyone claps, and then you sit down to do it yourself and discover the nine things nobody mentioned. This site is the nine things.

What's here

  • Guides — the full build from the videos, including the parts that failed first.
  • Claude Skills — drop-in folders that teach Claude a repeatable job.
  • Knowledge Bases — reference packs structured the way retrieval actually works.
  • Business Plans — complete plans and the templates behind them.

Free vs. paid

Anything I can give away, I give away — no email wall in front of it. The paid things are the ones that took weeks of work to get right, and they're priced so that one use pays for them.
