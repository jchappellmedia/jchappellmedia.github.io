What the exam covers

    How to use it

    Take it like the real thing

    The timed exam mixes all domains in random order. You can flag questions, jump around with the question list, and change answers until you submit. Shortcuts: A–D to answer, N and P to move, F to flag.

    Then study what you missed

    Your score report breaks results down by domain and question type. Study mode lets you drill one topic at a time, or only the questions you got wrong, with the explanation right after each answer.

    Your progress stays with you

    An exam in progress is saved in this browser, and the clock pauses when you leave the tab, so you can finish later. Nothing is sent anywhere.
